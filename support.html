Common questions

How do I join my bar?
Ask a General Manager for an invite code, create your account, and enter the code.

I am not getting notifications.
Check Profile → Notifications in the App, then your phone's Settings → Notifications → BarBeam. Notifications need to be allowed in both places.

How do I ask for time off?
Profile → Time off. A General Manager approves or declines it.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete my account. You can also email us from your account's email address.

Something looks wrong with my schedule.
Your managers control the schedule. Ask them first; they can fix shifts, jobs and time off. If the App itself is misbehaving, email us.
